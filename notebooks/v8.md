# v8: Early-Site Causal Tracing — How It Works

A conceptual companion to `v8.ipynb`. This doc explains *what* the notebook
does and *how to read its graph*, not how it's coded.

## The Big Idea

Causal tracing asks one question: **which layers of a transformer store a
given fact?**

The notebook is a self-contained, batch version of the ROME-style causal
tracing experiment. It loads several models, runs the same trace on each, and
picks a **robust causal region** per model — the layer band where factual
recall lives. The point is to locate, *without editing anything*, where a fact
like "The Eiffel Tower is in ___ (Paris)" sits in the weights, so a later
knowledge-editing method (ROME) knows which layer to touch.

## The Three Runs

For each fact the model is run three ways:

1. **Clean run** — feed the prompt normally. The model predicts the correct
   target token with probability `P_clean`.
2. **Corrupt run** — add Gaussian noise to the **subject-token embeddings**
   (e.g. the "Eiffel Tower" tokens). The subject is scrambled and the model
   "forgets" the fact: probability drops to `P_corrupt`.
3. **Restore run** — keep the subject corrupted, *but* at one layer window,
   overwrite the MLP output at the **last subject-token position** with the
   clean (uncorrupted) value. Measure `P_restore`.

From these:

- **Total effect** = `P_clean − P_corrupt` — how much the corruption hurt
  recall. This is the full "damage" available to be undone.
- **Indirect effect (IE)** of a window = `P_restore − P_corrupt` — how much
  restoring that one layer band brought the answer back.

A high IE means that band carries the fact. A near-zero IE means restoring it
does nothing; the fact doesn't live there.

### Why the "early site"?

Restoration happens at the **last subject token** position — the "early site"
in ROME terminology. This is where the MLP is believed to *write* the
subject→object association. (The "late site" would be the final prompt token
where the answer is read out.) This notebook isolates the early site.

## Windows

Instead of probing one layer at a time, restoration is done over a **window**
of consecutive layers (default 10 layers — the "canonical ROME" window). The
window slides across the model: for each center layer `c`, layers `[c−5, c+5)`
are jointly restored at the subject's last token.

- **Full-width windows** (the 10-layer windows that fit fully inside the
  model) are the ones that count toward selection.
- **Partial boundary windows** near layer 0 or the last layer can't form a
  full window. They're shown in gray as diagnostics only.

## Noise

- Gaussian, scaled to `3 ×` the embedding-weight std, added to every
  subject-token embedding.
- **10 noise samples** per fact are averaged, so each window's IE is a mean
  over 10 noisy runs. This dampens per-sample luck.

## Discovery → Confirmation Split

To guard against overfitting to one set of facts, the traced facts are split
in half:

1. **Discovery set** (50%) — used to *find* candidate causal regions.
2. **Confirmation set** (50%) — used to *validate* those regions on facts the
  discovery never saw.

A region must survive both halves to be selected.

## Heuristics & Gates

### Per-fact admission (only "reliable" facts get traced)
- **Clean must be correct** — the model's top clean prediction must equal the
  target. No point tracing a fact the model doesn't know.
- **Minimum total effect** (0.03) — corruption must actually damage recall.
  If scrambling the subject barely moves the probability, the fact isn't a
  clean editing target.
- **Stable corruption** — the 10 corrupt probabilities must not vary too
  wildly relative to the total effect.

### Discovery candidate selection — "local robust support"
A layer center becomes a discovery candidate only if:
- its 95% bootstrap CI **lower bound is above 0** (the effect is real, not
  sampling noise),
- its **median** and **trimmed mean** IE are both positive (not driven by a
  few outlier facts),
- **and** its neighbors (within radius 2) are also strong — specifically the
  neighbor-average trimmed-mean IE is at least **90%** of the best
  neighbor-average in the whole model.

That last condition is what kills isolated spikes: a lone towering layer with
flat neighbors fails it.

Contiguous candidate centers are then grouped into **regions**.

### Isolated-peak rule
A region with fewer than **3** supported centers is treated as **diagnostic
only** — it's drawn on the graph but never selected. Single-layer spikes are
not trusted as robust causal sites. (Loosen with `ALLOW_ISOLATED_REGIONS=True`
for diagnostics.)

### Confirmation gates
Each discovery region is re-checked on the confirmation set:
- The region's mean IE must have a **positive 95% CI** on held-out facts.
- The region must be **non-inferior** to the discovery raw peak — within 10%
  of it. I.e. it recovers the fact about as well as the single best layer
  found during discovery.

Regions passing both are ranked by confirmation median IE, trimmed mean,
pairwise win-rate, and CI lower bound. Rank 1 is the **confirmed robust
causal region**.

### Representative center
Inside the winning region, the center that most consistently beats its
siblings in pairwise per-fact comparisons (highest win-rate, then median IE)
is picked as the **representative trace center** — a single "best layer"
summary you could hand to ROME.

## The raw peak is diagnostic only

The single layer with the highest mean IE on the discovery set (the "raw
peak") is **not** trusted as the answer. It's drawn as a reference but never
selected. The actual selection comes from the robust-region logic above,
which demands neighbor support, held-out confirmation, and positive CIs.

## What the Graph Shows

One figure is produced per model (`early_site_causal_region_with_config_reference.png`).

- **X axis**: window center layer (`0 … num_layers−1`).
- **Left Y axis**: indirect effect.
- **Right Y axis**: normalized recovery = `mean_IE / total_effect` — what
  fraction of the corruption damage this window undoes.

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Light gray bars | Partial boundary windows (can't form a full 10-layer window) — diagnostic only. |
| Steel-blue bars | **Confirmation mean IE** per window — the main signal. |
| Blue shaded band | 95% bootstrap CI around the confirmation mean IE. |
| Black dashed line | Discovery mean IE — the shape found on the training half. |
| Orange line (○) | Confirmation **trimmed mean** IE (10% trim, robust to outlier facts). |
| Green line (□) | Confirmation **median** IE (fully robust to outliers). |
| Red line (right axis) | Normalized recovery diagnostic — fraction of total effect recovered. |
| Black dotted vertical | Discovery **raw peak** — single best layer on the discovery set; diagnostic only. |
| Purple dash-dot vertical | Config reference layer (from `src/config/model/*.yaml`) — drawn purely for visual comparison; **never** influences tracing or selection. |
| Gold shaded span | The **confirmed robust causal region** — the selected answer. |
| Crimson vertical | The **representative trace center** — the single best layer inside the gold span. |

### How to read a typical curve

A healthy trace looks like a **hill**: IE is near zero in the very early and
very late layers, and rises into a clear plateau somewhere in the
early-to-middle of the model — the early-site band where ROME-style edits
belong. The exact location varies a lot by architecture (some models peak
around layer 3, others around layer 18). What matters is that:

- the blue CI band stays **well above zero** across the plateau,
- the orange (trimmed) and green (median) robust lines **agree** with the blue
  bars — no single outlier fact is propping up the hill,
- and the gold span marks the heart of that plateau.

Warning signs:
- **Discovery (black dashed) and confirmation (blue bars) disagree** → the
  discovery peak didn't hold up; no robust region may be selected.
- **A single towering spike with flat neighbors** → an isolated peak; drawn
  but not selected (diagnostic only).
- **The whole curve hugs zero** → corruption didn't damage recall (facts the
  model doesn't strongly know), or the early site isn't the right site for
  this model.

crimson line is the single layer you'd hand to ROME as the edit layer.

## Output

Per model the notebook writes, among others:
- `discovery_windows.csv` / `confirmation_windows.csv` — per-layer IE, CI,
  neighbor support, and candidate flags.
- `confirmation_regions.csv` — the grouped regions and their confirmation
  stats.
- `final_selection.json` — the chosen robust region, its layer union, and the
  representative center.
- `early_site_causal_region_with_config_reference.png` — the figure above.
- A cross-model `batch_summary_*.csv` comparing all models side by side.
